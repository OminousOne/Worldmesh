## What you get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Your game, as plugins" icon="pencil">
    Worldmesh does the networking and none of the game. You add components, commands,
    actions, events and systems in plugins. No servers, no sockets, no routing in your code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Split by load, not by map" icon="random">
    Server Entity Groups simulate entities wherever they stand. Spatial Layer Cells own
    areas and answer "what's near me?". Scale either one, while the world runs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Actions land anywhere" icon="rocket">
    An arrow to an entity on another machine, mid-handoff, lands exactly once on the next
    tick. The target's side checks it with positions it can trust.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Smooth in the browser" icon="laptop">
    `@worldmesh/client` interpolates everyone else and predicts your own entity bit for
    bit with your movement rules, adapting to the connection.
  </Card>
  <Card title="See inside" icon="magnifier">
    `@worldmesh/debug` shows who simulates what and whose area is whose, scales the world,
    adds fake lag per server, and draws every message on the bus.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Test without servers" icon="approve-check">
    `TestWorld` runs a whole world in one process, tick by tick, with real groups and
    cells passing real messages.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
